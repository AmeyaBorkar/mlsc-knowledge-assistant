MLSC Hackathons

MLSC organizes and participates in hackathons to encourage students to build practical solutions.

Hackathons may be conducted independently or in collaboration with external organizations.

Participants generally progress through several stages including registration, idea submission, shortlisting, development and final presentation.

During development, mentors may provide technical guidance to participating teams.

MLSC encourages participants to focus on solving meaningful problems rather than building projects solely for demonstration.

Projects may involve multiple technical domains. For example, an AI-powered application could require artificial intelligence, web development and cloud deployment.

Participants are generally evaluated based on factors such as innovation, technical implementation, problem relevance, feasibility, scalability and quality of presentation.

The exact judging criteria can vary between hackathons.
