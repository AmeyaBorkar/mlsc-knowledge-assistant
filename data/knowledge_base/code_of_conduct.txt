MLSC Code of Conduct

MLSC members are expected to maintain a respectful and inclusive environment.

Members should communicate respectfully with other students, coordinators, leads, mentors and external collaborators.

Harassment, discrimination, plagiarism and intentional disruption of community activities are not acceptable.

Members should respect intellectual property and should properly acknowledge external resources used in projects.

Technical disagreements should be handled professionally.

Members are encouraged to ask questions and challenge ideas constructively.

Violation of the code of conduct may result in removal from specific activities or other actions determined by the MLSC leadership team.
