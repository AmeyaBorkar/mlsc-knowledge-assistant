MLSC Membership

Students can participate in MLSC activities as community members.

Membership allows students to participate in technical events, workshops, hackathons and community initiatives.

Being a member does not automatically make a student a coordinator.

Coordinator positions are selected through the MLSC recruitment process.

Students interested in becoming coordinators are generally evaluated on their technical skills, willingness to contribute, communication and ability to work in a team.

Members are encouraged to attend technical sessions and participate in projects before applying for leadership or coordinator roles.

Students do not need to be experts in every technology to participate in MLSC.
