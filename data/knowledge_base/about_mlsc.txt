MLSC VIT Pune — About the Community

Microsoft Learn Student Community (MLSC) VIT Pune is a student-led technical community focused on helping students learn, build, collaborate and participate in technology-related activities.

The community conducts technical workshops, hackathons, study sessions, project-building initiatives and networking activities throughout the academic year.

MLSC aims to create an environment where students can learn technologies through practical implementation rather than only theoretical study.

The community is open to students interested in areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, web development, application development, cloud computing and emerging technologies.

Students can participate in MLSC as members, coordinators, domain leads or through event-specific volunteering opportunities.

The community encourages members to participate in hackathons, technical competitions, open-source projects and collaborative development.

MLSC also works with external technology communities, industry professionals and student organizations for selected events and initiatives.

The primary objective of MLSC is not only to conduct events but also to build a community of students who learn from one another and create meaningful technical projects.
