MLSC Technical Domains

MLSC has multiple technical domains through which students can learn and contribute to projects.

The major domains include:

1. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
2. Web Development
3. App Development
4. Cloud Computing
5. Web3

Each domain has domain leads who are responsible for planning technical activities and coordinating with coordinators.

The AI/ML domain focuses on machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, computer vision, generative AI and applied AI systems.

The Web Development domain focuses on frontend development, backend development, APIs, databases and full-stack applications.

The App Development domain focuses on mobile application development and related technologies.

The Cloud Computing domain focuses on cloud infrastructure, deployment, DevOps, containers and cloud-native applications.

The Web3 domain focuses on blockchain technologies, decentralized applications, smart contracts and related technologies.

Domain activities can include workshops, internal projects, study sessions, technical discussions and hackathon preparation.
